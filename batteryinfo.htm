Low-Level Battery Info

Low-Level Battery Info is free Windows software designed to retrieve and display detailed battery information through low-level data access methods.

The software provides battery identification, capacity monitoring, voltage readings, power status, estimated runtime, battery health calculations, anomaly indicators, and raw device data.

Low-Level Battery Info 1.5 combines battery identification, capacity monitoring, health calculations, power measurements, runtime reporting, anomaly indicators, and low-level raw-data inspection.

Its main advantage is the combination of straightforward battery monitoring with additional technical information for advanced users and software developers.

Whether used for routine monitoring or troubleshooting, the software helps users examine reported battery characteristics and investigate potential irregularities without treating a single software reading as definitive proof of hardware health.
