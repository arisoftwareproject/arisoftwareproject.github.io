#Troubleshooting and Diagnostic Guidance

Observation Possible explanation or next step
Full Charge Capacity is significantly below Design Capacity The battery may have experienced capacity degradation.
Current Capacity decreases rapidly Check system power consumption and compare repeated readings.
Voltage appears unusual Verify the units, source data, device specifications, and raw response structure.
Charging status does not match the physical connection Check the reported adapter status, battery interface, and driver behavior.
Estimated Runtime is unavailable The driver may not provide a runtime estimate.
Some identification fields are missing The battery firmware or driver may not expose those fields.
A raw query fails Check interface availability, query support, and the returned status.
The laptop shuts down unexpectedly Investigate battery condition, voltage behavior under load, power connections, and possible hardware faults.

Low-Level Battery Info can assist with these investigations, but software readings alone cannot conclusively diagnose every battery failure.
