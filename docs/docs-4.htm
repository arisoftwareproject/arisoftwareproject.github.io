#Current Capacity

**Current Capacity** displays the amount of energy currently reported as remaining in the battery.

Example:

`29230 mWh (98.31%)`

The value consists of two measurements:

- **29,230 mWh:** The reported remaining battery energy.
- **98.31%:** The remaining energy relative to the reported full-charge capacity.

The software uses milliwatt-hours (mWh), a unit of energy.

This differs from milliampere-hours (mAh), which measures electric charge. The two units cannot be directly compared without considering voltage.

Current capacity changes during battery operation and may fluctuate depending on the battery controller's reporting behavior.
